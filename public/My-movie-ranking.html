The Best Movies According to Samuel

My Top Movies of all-time

Attack On Titan

This has to be one of the greatest anime ever made. This anime literally had me feeling all emotions, from happy to sad to angry. The ending even made me cry. The animation for most part of the anime is top notch

Naruto

This is another anime that I rate so highly. It taught me that hardwork really does pay off. Never give up even if it seems hard as long as you keep believing. The fight scenes were awesome. The anime really teaches you about life to some extent and the villains has so much aura

Avengers Endgame

This is the peak of superhero movies for me. Everything here was on the line. One of my favourite characters even died. They were so many great action scenes in this movie and a lot of star actors in one movie makes you really excited
